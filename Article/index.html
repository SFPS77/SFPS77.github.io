The ICAO finally reveals the next location for the Aviation History Festival

Last Friday, at the ICAO Center in Poland, Oporto was announced as the host city for the 2027 Aviation History Festival

The Aviation History Festival is an international festival that happens every 2 years, where airlines from all over the world gather to show their evolution throughout the years. They also show their next projects and improvements.

In 2025, the host was Munich, Germany. For 2027, the chosen city is Oporto in Portugal:

Oporto has everything an aviation festival needs: a proud history of flight, a stunning riverfront for the crowds, and a community that welcomes visitors like family. We chose it because it looks to the past and the future at the same time. - Roberta Krol, The ICAO Council President

At every festival, 3 airplanes are chosen to be displayed as the main attraction. This year's selection of planes is one of the best ever. It will include the A380, the A320 and the DHC-6 Twin Otter.
